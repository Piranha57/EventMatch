# EventMatch
EventMatch is a community‑driven web platform designed to help people discover and participate in local cultural and religious events such as melas, bhandaras, jagratas, food fairs, and festivals. 
It bridges tradition and technology by connecting attendees, organizers and local businesses in one vibrant space.  
Users can easily search events near them, explore detailed schedules, mark themselves as Interested or Going, and even find companions through the Find Buddy feature — matching people with similar interests and locations. 
Organizers can create and manage events, post updates, and view engagement analytics, while the Festival Calendar keeps everyone informed about upcoming celebrations.  
With its intuitive interface, responsive design, and focus on community connection, EventMatch transforms how people experience local gatherings — making every event more accessible, social, and memorable.
